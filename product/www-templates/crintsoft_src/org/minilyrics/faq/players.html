  • Spotify

My favorite player isn't supported

The table below listed the players that isn't supported. If the SDK of your favorite player can be found, and it's popular enough (Google search must return more than 500,000 results), please contact us.

Player Name The reason of unsupported
Gom Player Can't get the SDK of the player
Karafun Player Can't get the SDK of the player
Virtual DJ SDK doesn't provide enough information to make a MiniLyrics plugin.
CyberLink PowerDVD Can't get the SDK of the player

XBMC Can't get the SDK of the player
