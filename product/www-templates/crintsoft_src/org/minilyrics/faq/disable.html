_TLM(How do I disable MiniLyrics to start with my Players?)

  • _TLM(Please right mouse button on MiniLyrics window)
  • _TLM(Click menu item: 'Preferences...')
  • _TLM(In the popup window, click 'System' > 'Startup' tab, choose the player you want MiniLyrics to start with.)
