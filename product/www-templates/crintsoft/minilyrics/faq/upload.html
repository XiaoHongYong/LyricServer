How do I upload lyrics and expand the lyrics database?

If you have made a .LRC or text lyrics file, and you want to share it with all the users of MiniLyrics, you can upload it to MiniLyrics lyrics server.

To upload lyrics, please follow the steps below:

  1. Click 'Lyrics' > 'Upload lyrics' > 'Upload Lyrics' to open 'Upload Lyrics' window.
  2. Browse the folder where your lyrics file are stored.
  3. Click onto the lyrics file you want to submit.
  4. Make sure the title, artist and album name are correct.
  5. Click on Upload button.
