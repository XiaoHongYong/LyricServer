How do I control the scrolling of text lyrics?

MiniLyrics could record and replay the scrolling action of text lyrics. You can just scroll lyrics with your keyboard or mouse wheel, and MiniLyrics will record them and replay them next time.

The UI below will be showed up when you have .TXT lyrics opened.
