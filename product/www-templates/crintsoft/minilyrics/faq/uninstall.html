Uninstall MiniLyrics

Uninstall MiniLyrics on Windows

  1. Click Windows start menu, then click 'All Programs' > 'MiniLyrics' > 'Uninstall MiniLyrics'.
  2. Click 'Uninstall' button in the popped up window to uninstall MiniLyrics.

Uninstall MiniLyrics on Mac

  1. Click Finder menu: 'Go' > 'Go to folder...'
  2. Input '~/Library/iTunes/iTunes Plug-ins', then click 'Go'.
  3. Move files to trash: 'MLPlugin.ini', 'MiniLyrics for iTunes.bundle'
  4. Move MiniLyrics.app to trash in Applications folder.
