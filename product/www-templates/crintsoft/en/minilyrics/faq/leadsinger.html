Why the lyrics get cut off half way through the song when playing them on the Leadsinger Mic?

Leadsinger Mic only supports ANSI encoding lyrics file, so you need to convert your lyrics files from unicode encoding to ANSI encoding.

You can download the 'File Encoding Converter' tool to do this boring job. Please click the link bellow to download:
http://www.crintsoft.com/download/FileEncConverter.zip
