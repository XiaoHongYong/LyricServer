Save embedded lyrics in song files by default

You can turn on saving embedded lyrics in song files by default in MiniLyrics preferences window.

  1. Open MiniLyrics Preferences window by clicking right mouse button on MiniLyrics window.
  2. Click 'Lyrics' tab.

  3. Check 'Save lyrics in song file'.
