No lyrics are displayed in MusicPlayer, why?

MusicPlayer searches lyrics by the artist and title name of songs. If no lyrics are displayed in MusicPlayer:

  1. Please make sure that the artist and title name of your song are correct.
  2. You can click 'Search Lyrics' button, and search lyrics manaully.
  3. Sometimes, there are no lyrics on MusicPlayer server, and if you have them, you could upload these lyrics to MusicPlayer server and share with other users.
  4. If you got any error messagess, please contact us for helping.
