No lyrics are displayed in MiniLyrics, why?

MiniLyrics searches lyrics by the artist and title name of songs. If no lyrics are displayed in MiniLyrics:

  1. Please make sure that the artist and title name of your song are correct.
  2. You can click 'Search Lyrics' button, and search lyrics manaully.
  3. Sometimes, there are no lyrics on MiniLyrics server, and if you have them, you could upload these lyrics to MiniLyrics server and share with other users.
  4. If you got any error messagess, please contact us for helping.
